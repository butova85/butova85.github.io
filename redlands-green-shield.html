GIS & Urban Planning Project

Redlands Green Shield 100K

A strategic GIS initiative to plan and map the planting of 100,000 trees in Redlands, CA. This project leverages 3D digital twins to create a sustainable "green shield" against rising urban temperatures.

View on GitHub

Project Objectives

The goal is to enhance the city's urban canopy through data-driven decision-making. By identifying high-heat zones and available planting areas, we can optimize the environmental impact of every new tree.

  • 3D Modeling: Created a digital twin of Redlands in ArcGIS Pro.
  • Heat Analysis: Used spatial data to identify "heat islands" requiring urgent canopy coverage.
  • Scalability: Designed a phased planting plan for 100,000 trees over a multi-year period.

Tools & Technologies

  • ArcGIS Pro & ArcGIS Online
  • 3D Scene Layers (Digital Twins)
  • Spatial Analysis Tools
  • Python for Task Automation
  • Data Validation & QA
