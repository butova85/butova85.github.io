Project Story & Progress

Redlands Green Shield 100K

This is a living document of my journey to plan and map the planting of 100,000 trees in Redlands, CA. As a resident and an engineer, I am using GIS to build a data-driven "green shield" for our community.

The Vision

Redlands is known for its greenery, but as temperatures rise, the urban canopy needs strategic expansion. My goal is to identify exactly where these 100,000 trees will have the most impact—reducing heat islands and improving air quality.

Project Updates & Progress

March 27, 2026

Identifying Planting Zones

I have started identifying specific planting zones by creating a 3D map of Redlands in ArcGIS. By layering current canopy data over infrastructure maps, I can see the "gaps" in our green shield.

March 22, 2026

Project Initiation

Defined the core project plan and structure. The focus is on using automation engineering principles to manage a massive environmental goal.

Technical Toolkit

To build this map and manage the data, I am utilizing:

  • ArcGIS Pro: 3D modeling and Digital Twins.
  • Python: Automating spatial data processing.
  • ArcGIS Online: Project visualization and sharing.
  • Spatial Analysis: Environmental impact modeling.
