Redlands Green Shield 100K

Project Overview

The Redlands Green Shield project is a comprehensive GIS-based initiative designed to plan, visualize, and track the planting of 100,000 trees in Redlands, California. The goal is to enhance the city's urban canopy and provide a "green shield" against rising temperatures.

Key Features

  • 3D City Modeling: Developed a digital twin of Redlands using ArcGIS to simulate tree growth and shade impact.
  • Spatial Analysis: Identified optimal planting zones based on soil quality, existing infrastructure, and heat map data.
  • Strategic Planning: Established a phased project plan for long-term sustainability and maintenance.

Tools Used

ArcGIS Pro, Python for automation, 3D Scene Layers.
